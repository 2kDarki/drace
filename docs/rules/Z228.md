# Z228 — Abstraction Leak

## What it targets

Returning raw internal mutable structures from functions.

## Why it exists

Leaking internals weakens encapsulation and makes invariants harder to protect.

## What it encourages

- Intentional return contracts
- Defensive copies when needed
- Domain-level abstractions over raw containers

## What it does NOT say

- It does not ban lists/dicts/sets
- It does not forbid performance-driven choices
- It flags interface risk, not guaranteed defects
